From photo to PDF in three easy steps.

Pdfoto gathers your pictures into one tidy PDF. Arrange them on pages any way you like, and copy out the written text whenever you need it. Everything happens on your device, and nothing is uploaded.

Get started
PDF
  1. Add your photosDrop in pictures of notes, handouts or documents from your device.
  2. Arrange your pagesCrop, rotate and stretch each photo. Drag it anywhere on a page, or move it onto another page.
  3. Save what you needDownload a PDF, or read the text from the photos you pick and save it as .txt or Word.
